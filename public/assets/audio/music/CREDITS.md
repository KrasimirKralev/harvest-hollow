# Harvest Hollow music: credits

Every track here is real, composed music by the artists below, shared under **CC0** (public domain dedication):
no attribution is legally required, and we credit every artist anyway, here and in the game (Settings > Credits).
The machine-readable list is `tracks.json` (the music director in `public/js/audio.js` and the Credits view both read
it; `test/audio-music.test.js` checks that this file names every track in it, and that both files of every track exist).
Each licence was checked on its source page on the date given.

FreePD.com (Kevin MacLeod's public-domain catalogue) was the first choice, but it closed in 2025 (the site now says
"we have officially taken the service offline"), so every track comes from OpenGameArt.org's CC0 music.

## How these files were made

Tools: a static ffmpeg build (`ffmpeg version 7.0.2-static https://johnvansickle.com/ffmpeg/`, from
<https://johnvansickle.com/ffmpeg/releases/ffmpeg-release-amd64-static.tar.xz>; it has `libvorbis` and the native `aac`
encoder). The originals and the WAV masters stay outside the repository (`.scratch/owner3/music/src/` and `master/`).

For every track:

1. Download the original (the "Original file" link above) into `src/`.
2. Measure it with the same filter chain and `loudnorm` in measuring mode (the values feed step 3):
   `ffmpeg -hide_banner -nostats -i src/<original> -map 0:a -af <chain>,loudnorm=I=-18.0:TP=-1.0:LRA=20:print_format=json -f null -`
   where `<chain>` is the part of step 3's `-af` before `loudnorm` (resample to 44.1 kHz stereo, trim, fade in and out).
3. Normalise linearly to -18 LUFS (Wooden Inn: -20 LUFS, which its peaks allow without a limiter), true peak at most
   -1 dBTP, into a 16-bit WAV master.
4. Encode the master twice: Ogg Vorbis at quality 0.6 (about 70 kbit/s; Chrome, Firefox, Android) and AAC-LC at
   64 kbit/s in MP4 with the index at the front (`+faststart`, so Safari can start playing while it streams).

The exact commands (steps 3 and 4), in the order they ran on 2026-10-04:


`key` in `tracks.json` (the pitch class the drag-pluck ladder plays along in) was estimated from each master with a
Krumhansl-Schmuckler key profile over an STFT chromagram; a minor key is given as its relative major (its
pentatonic notes are the same).

Budget: the 16 files total 17.65 MB (16.84 MiB); the test keeps the total at or under 20 MB.
